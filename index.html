TV Energy Consumption

Energy consumption for different TV screen types and sizes

Most TVs on sale in Australia are LED, and they vary considerably in energy consumption. Use the buttons to compare screen technologies.

Energy consumption by star rating

Each dot is one TV model, coloured by screen technology. Higher star ratings generally mean lower energy use. Hover over a dot to see its screen size.
